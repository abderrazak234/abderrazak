Frequently Asked Questions

What age is this coloring book for?

It is designed for toddlers ages 1–3, with simple illustrations and large coloring areas. Adult supervision is recommended.

What paper size should I use?

The pages are designed for standard US Letter paper (8.5 × 11 inches).

Can I print the pages at home?

Yes. Once you have access to the PDF, you can print the pages on a home printer.

How do I access the complete book?

Follow the instructions shown when you choose to unlock the book. Availability depends on the offers presented to you.

Is this a physical book?

No. This is a digital printable PDF, not a physical book shipped to your home.
